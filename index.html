03 — How I work

From fog to launch.

01

Discover

Understand the problem before defining the solution: users, stakeholders, constraints and business goals.

In practiceStakeholder interviews and user research, consolidated into personas, a pain-point map and agreed success criteria.
02

Frame

Translate insights into a prioritised, executable scope with explicit trade-offs and measurable outcomes.

In practiceProblem statements, user journeys and a prioritised backlog (MoSCoW, impact vs. effort), with decisions and rationale recorded in Confluence.
03

Prototype

Make it real early. AI-assisted HTML prototypes turn an idea into a working screen in hours, not weeks.

In practiceInteractive HTML prototypes generated with AI and run locally, refined with stakeholders and tested with users before engineering capacity is committed.
04

Deliver

Predictable delivery through clear priorities, transparent progress and close work with engineering.

In practiceThe validated prototype is broken down into tasks in a task-management tool and built together with backend and frontend developers, with regular stakeholder reporting and clean handovers.
